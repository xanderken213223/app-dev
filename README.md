# Breaking Bad

## My Favorite Series

**Breaking Bad** is my favorite TV series. It is about Walter White, a chemistry teacher who starts making illegal drugs after discovering that he has cancer. He works with his former student, Jesse Pinkman.

### Why I Like It

*I like Breaking Bad because it has an exciting story, great characters, and unexpected events.*

### My Favorite Characters

- **Walter White** - A chemistry teacher who becomes a drug manufacturer.
- **Jesse Pinkman** - Walter's former student and partner.
- **Saul Goodman** - A lawyer who helps Walter and Jesse.
- **Hank Schrader** - A DEA agent and Walter's brother-in-law.

### My Favorite Things

1. The interesting storyline.
2. The amazing acting.
3. The unexpected plot twists.
4. The exciting scenes.

### My Favorite Quote

> I am the one who knocks!

### My Rating

I give this series a `10/10` because it is exciting and entertaining.
